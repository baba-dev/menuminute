## SaaS refund and cancellation notice — 2 November 2026

The published, indexable `/refund-policy` replaces the draft `(information)/[slug]` placeholder. The policy applies to **BabaBuilds platform software charges**, not independent merchants' food sales. Its default **no discretionary change-of-mind, unused-time, or early-cancellation refunds** has legally mandatory exceptions for material service deficiency/misdescription, non-delivery, duplicate or incorrect charges, and legally required payment reversals. Subscribers can request cancellation of future renewals; the implemented Razorpay route schedules period-end cancellation for eligible subscriptions. No new payment processor/refund issuance workflow is created by this legal-page PR.

The pricing page shows a free QR menu plus `₹119/month` Ordering and `₹499/month` Kitchen Pro. Its buttons offer purchase only when the deployment's checkout switches are on (otherwise a free start or an early-access request); see [commercial availability](../commercial-availability.md). `docs/operations/razorpay-billing.md` says paid checkout is **off by default** pending provider approval. The policy avoids asserting that any merchant can currently buy these plans, that every billing issue is resolved within an invented deadline, or that a voluntary refund is a statutory obligation.
