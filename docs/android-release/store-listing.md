# Play Store listing — review draft

**App title:** MenuMinute Restaurant Manager

**Short description:** Manage restaurant menus, orders, POS and kitchen in one place.

**Description:**
Manage your restaurant from a phone with MenuMinute. Publish a permanent QR menu, update dishes and availability, manage guest orders, use your existing POS workspace and coordinate the kitchen. Your staff access remains scoped to your restaurant and role.

Features depend on your restaurant's existing plan and enabled services. All live order, POS and kitchen actions require a network connection. Guests can use your restaurant QR menu without installing this app.

This Play edition does not sell subscriptions in the app. If you already use MenuMinute on the web, sign in to access your active restaurant features.
