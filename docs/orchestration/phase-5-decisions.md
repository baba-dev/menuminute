Each Open decision gets: scenario + alternative options, product-owner-approved choice or explicitly deferred scope, affected service contract/fixture version, migration impact and region/entitlement rollout gate. A blocked decision does not stop unrelated architecture preparation or isolated view work, but prevents enabling the affected live flow. Reconcile existing [#8](https://github.com/baba-dev/menuminute/issues/8) rather than modifying its approved predecessor text without explicit coordination.

Suggested compact fixtures:

- Restaurant 18:00–02:00, submission 23:45, pending expires next-day 02:00; a staff attempt at deadline races with expiry using database clock. Accepted work continues.
- Restaurant open 24 hours needs approved business-day cutoff, not a guessed midnight.
- Cross-currency examples for zero-decimal JPY, two-decimal INR, three-decimal OMR; no sum across currencies, preserve order quote/snapshot precision. Tax calculation for each rollout region is blocked until separately approved.
- Two-station dine-in: one item to grill, one to drinks, expo sees both; grill complete alone cannot transition full order to Ready. Duplicate accepted outbox replay must not create a second grill ticket. After reconnect, all stations reconstruct exactly once from PostgreSQL.
- Public QR printed before rename/menu edit/downgrade remains usable. Guest session expiry cannot revoke QR; explicit QR regeneration revokes old token.
- Guest receives uncertain POST result, reconciles original key before new request; changed-item hash is conflict, additional round uses a new key and separate ticket.
